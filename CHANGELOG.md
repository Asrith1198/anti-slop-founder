# Changelog

All notable changes to this skill are documented here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-02

Initial public release.

### Added
- `SKILL.md` orchestrator covering three phases: Idea, Brand, Validate.
- `idea-discovery.md` — founder-edge mapping, market-signal reading, problem-mining, scoring and convergence.
- `idea-validation.md` — competitor/market research branched by 8 business types, each with its own sources, pain signals, and compliance flag; four-axis scoring (Severity/Frequency/Market Whitespace/TAM); validation-integrity checklist.
- `survey-design.md` — bias-checked survey design, build/distribution paths, and a framework for analyzing returned response data.
- `naming.md` — SMILE/SCRATCH naming methodology by vertical, plus a real availability & trademark search step.
- `style-archetypes.md` — 12 visual style archetypes (Corporate Systematic through Luxury Minimal).
- `color-palettes.md`, `typography.md`, `logo-concepts.md` — hex-coded palettes, font pairings, and logo construction approaches per archetype.
- `messaging.md` — value proposition, elevator pitch, tagline, messaging pillars, and tone of voice per archetype.
- `brandbook.md` — compiling everything into one final document and choosing its output format.
- `anti-ai-slop.md` — visual, verbal, and structural clichés checked against every output.
- `examples/` — a full worked example (fictional idea) across validation, survey, and brand book.

### Design decisions
- Branding happens before validation by default, but the skill flags that work as provisional until validation runs, and loops back to the next idea candidate on a weak verdict.
- Validation research is branched by business type rather than defaulting to a SaaS/startup playbook for every idea.
