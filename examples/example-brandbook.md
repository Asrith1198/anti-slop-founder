# Example: Brand Book

*Fictional worked example, completing the QueueCut idea from `example-validation.md` and `example-survey.md`. Invented brand, used only to show the output format `brandbook.md` produces.*

## QueueCut
**"Order ahead. Skip the line."**
*Archetype: Playful Edtech / Student*

## The one-liner
QueueCut helps engineering students get their canteen food without losing a third of their lunch break to a queue, by letting them order and pay ahead for a set pickup time — unlike general food-delivery apps, which don't serve single-campus canteens at all.

## Elevator pitch
Students at single-canteen campuses lose 15-20 minutes of a 45-minute lunch break standing in line — sometimes skipping lunch entirely rather than deal with it. QueueCut lets them order ahead from their phone and walk straight to a pickup counter at a set time, no queue, no cash confusion, no friends holding four spots. It's not another delivery app — it's built for the one queue every student on this campus already stands in every day.

## Who it's for
Engineering students on a single, shared canteen schedule — starting with one campus as a pilot, not a broad multi-campus launch.

## Visual identity

**Palette**
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Primary | `#58CC02` |
| Secondary | `#1CB0F6` |
| Accent | `#FF9600` (+ `#FF4B4B` for urgency states, e.g. "order closing soon") |
| Neutral-Dark | `#3C3C3C` |
| Neutral-Light | `#F7F7F7` |
| Background | `#FFFFFF` |

**Typography:** Heading — Baloo 2 (rounded, friendly, high-energy). Body — Nunito Sans.

**Logo direction:** A mascot named **Zip** — a small rounded lightning-bolt character, personality: perpetually a little too eager, practically vibrating with impatience to get you fed faster. Face-only version works as the app icon; full-body version (mid-zoom, motion lines) for splash/loading screens.

## Voice
Warm, encouraging, a little cheeky — matches the Playful Edtech/Student archetype. Says: *"Your tray's basically already in your hands."* Doesn't say: corporate "streamlined dining solutions" language.

**Messaging pillars:**
1. **Built for one queue, not every queue** — reason to believe: single-campus focus, not a generic delivery app → rules out: being one more app competing with Swiggy/Zomato on breadth.
2. **No more spot-holding fights** — reason to believe: directly pulled from survey data (two independent respondents named this unprompted) → rules out: being read as a minor convenience feature rather than solving a real daily friction.

## Validation snapshot
**Verdict:** Proceed — single-campus pilot first. **Thesis:** engineering students on a shared lunch window lose real time to a queue no existing app is scoped to solve. **Score read:** frequency strong, whitespace strong, severity moderate-to-strong (survey surfaced a skipped-meals data point), TAM weak-to-moderate until proven across more than one campus.

## Status
**Validated for a single-campus pilot** — not yet validated for multi-campus scale. The brand work above is final for the pilot; revisit before expanding to a second campus.
